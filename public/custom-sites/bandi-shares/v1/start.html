The basics

Nine questions every new trader asks.

Tap a question to read the answer.

1. What is a financial market?

A place where people buy and sell things like currencies, gold, shares and bonds. Prices change all day as buyers and sellers agree on what something is worth.

2. What is trading?

Buying or selling in a market to try to profit from a change in price. You can make money, and you can lose money.

3. Why does price move?

Because people change their minds about what something is worth. News, interest rates, inflation and the economy change those opinions, and big investors move money as a result.

4. What is technical analysis?

Reading the price chart to find patterns and levels that help you decide when to enter and exit a trade.

5. What is fundamental analysis?

Studying the reasons behind price moves: the economy, interest rates, inflation and world events. Professionals call these "macroeconomic drivers".

6. Why understand both?

Fundamentals help explain where price is likely to go and why. The chart helps you choose a good moment to act. Together they give you a reason and a plan.

7. What is risk management?

Deciding how much you are willing to lose on a trade before you open it, so that one bad trade never ruins your account.

8. What is trading psychology?

How your emotions affect your decisions. Fear and greed make traders break their own rules. Good psychology means following your plan.

9. What should I learn first?

Start with the basics of fundamentals: what moves markets and why. Then add the chart, risk management and psychology. Our 7-Day Fundamentals Bootcamp is built for exactly this first step.
