About KaiSync Institution

An AI academy. Recordings first. You choose the course.

KaiSync Institution teaches practical AI — websites, automation, marketing, and applications — as an open catalog, not a lock-step term.

Our mission

Closing the gap between curiosity and capability.

KaiSync Institution exists because too many people who are curious about AI never get the chance to actually build with it. We publish five recorded courses — Basic AI; Build a website, beginning to end; Automation with Zapier, n8n, or Power Automate; Marketing with AI; and Applications. One subscription opens all of them. You pick what you want. You can sit in several at once. New to AI? Start with Basic AI. You do not have to.

How the academy runs

Catalog, plans, then showcase.

Open catalog

Library R99, Scholar R249, and Studio R499 all include every recorded course. Pick any. Join more than one.

Seminars and one on one sessions

Every plan includes two AI seminars a week. Studio adds a Sunday business seminar and one free 45-minute one on one session a month. Extra one on one sessions are R49 per 45 minutes — submit your problem when you book.

Studio showcase

Studio students submit a project each month. We pick the top two, feature and market them on the website, LinkedIn and Facebook, and guide the builder through next steps. Builder 90%, academy 10%.

Companies and pathways

Teams can train. Top students can teach.

A company can register a group for the catalog, or book an on-site workshop on how to use AI at work. Each year KaiSync Institution invites the top two students to help teach seminars. Under-18 students can be showcased with a guardian — they cannot sell a project alone.

  • Student plans — Library R99, Scholar R249, Studio R499
  • Company seats from R199 per person a month and workshops from R3,500 — see For Business
  • Marketing with AI is its own course, not a hidden extra on every plan
