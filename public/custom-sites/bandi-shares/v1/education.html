Questions

Before you enrol.

What is the macro approach and how is it different?

Our macro approach is rooted in fundamental economics: central bank policy, yield curves, CPI, GDP and global capital flows. Unlike technical pattern-chasing, we read the economic data driving markets before the chart reacts.

Why does the educational program run for 6 months?

Macroeconomic mastery isn't a weekend skill. Six months gives us time to walk through full policy cycles, multiple data releases and live regime shifts, with the repetition required to internalise the framework.

Are split-payment options available?

Yes. The 6-Month Educational Program (R6,000) can be paid in split payments. The split-payment option is available on the program's product link.

Do I need an economics background?

No. The framework distils macroeconomic concepts into clear, actionable lessons. If you can read a chart, you can learn to read the economy driving it.

Is this a signal service?

No. Bandi Shares is an educational platform. Trade discussions provide context and rationale, not blind signals. The goal is to make you a self-sufficient macro trader.

What is the refund policy?

All sales of digital products and educational programs are final once access is granted. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. We encourage you to start with the free community.
