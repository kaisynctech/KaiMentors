FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I get all five courses on every plan?

Yes. Library, Scholar, and Studio all open the full catalog. Pick any recording. Join more than one at the same time.

What's the difference between the plans?

Library is self-paced recordings. Scholar adds one live lab and one marked assignment a month. Studio adds two live labs, three project clinic reports, and eligibility for the public showcase. No plan includes unlimited 1:1 tutoring.

How does the Studio showcase work?

Studio only. Up to two projects a month are featured on the website and can be shared on LinkedIn and Facebook. Companies enquire from the page. If they buy, the builder keeps 90% and KaiSync Institution keeps 10%. Under-18 students need a guardian for any sale.

What payment methods are accepted?

Card payments through PayFast, in South African Rand. Enrolment is month-to-month.

Can I cancel or change plan?

Yes. All plans are billed monthly with no lock-in. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your dashboard.

Can a company train a team?

Yes. Email kaisynctech@gmail.com about group seats or an on-site AI workshop. Student plans stay at R99 / R149 / R249.
