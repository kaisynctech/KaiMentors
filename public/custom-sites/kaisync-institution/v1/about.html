About KaiSync Institution

A worldwide school for learning and building with AI.

We teach AI to anyone, anywhere: people starting from zero, builders who want to ship real products, and companies training their teams. Everything we teach ends in something you can actually use.

Our mission

Make AI something you use, not just something you read about.

AI is changing every job and every industry, yet most people only ever see the headlines. Few get the chance to sit down and build with it.

KaiSync Institution exists to close that gap. We are an online school focused on one subject, AI, and we teach it the practical way: you learn a tool, then you use it to build a website, an automation, a campaign or an app.

Our classes are online, so it does not matter where you live. If you have an internet connection and the curiosity, you have a seat.

What makes us different

Built around doing, not watching.

Online and worldwide

Recorded courses you can open at any hour, plus live seminars you can join from any country.

Every course ends in a build

You leave each course with a real project for your portfolio, your business or a client.

The tools the industry uses

Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Lovable and more. We update what we teach as the field moves.

For people and companies

Individuals learn at their own pace. Companies train whole teams with workshops and seats.
