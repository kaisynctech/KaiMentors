About Bandi Shares.

Who Bandile is, what Bandi Shares does, and how it can help you as a trader.

BandileFounder, Bandi SharesEconomist & trader
Who is Bandile?

An economist and trader who started where most traders start.

Bandile's story begins in Mpuluzi, Mayflower. He studied Quantitative Risk Management Sciences at North-West University, where he was first exposed to financial markets.

In 2017 he started trading with technical analysis. He made money and lost money, but could not create consistency. He left university to trade, then returned to education and studied Economic Sciences at Wits.

That is where the missing piece came together. Today he combines macroeconomics, fundamentals, sentiment, capital flows and risk with technical execution.

What is Bandi Shares?

Trading education that starts with why markets move.

Most traders are taught to read the chart: candlesticks, support and resistance, indicators. Very few are taught what actually moves price: interest rates, inflation, economic growth, big investors and world events.

Bandi Shares was created to give retail traders access to the part of trading Bandile wishes he had understood much earlier. The mission is to bridge academic economics and retail trading, so every trader understands the reason behind the move, not just the reaction on the chart.
