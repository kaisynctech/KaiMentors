What fundamental analysis means

Fundamental analysis, in plain language.

It means studying what drives price: interest rates, inflation, economic growth, the decisions of big investors and world events. For example, interest rates change where investors want to put their money, and that moves currencies, gold and shares.

?Why did gold rise when the news looked bad?
?Why did the rand fall after a US jobs report?
?Why do markets sometimes ignore good news?
The chart shows the reaction. Fundamentals help explain the reason behind the reaction.
