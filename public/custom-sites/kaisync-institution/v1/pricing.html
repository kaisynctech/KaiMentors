FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I get all five courses on every plan?

Yes. Library, Scholar, and Studio all open the full catalog. Pick any recording. Join more than one at the same time.

What's the difference between the plans?

Library is recordings plus two AI seminars a week. Scholar adds one marked assignment a month. Studio adds the Sunday business seminar, one free 45-minute one on one session a month, and eligibility for the public showcase. Extra one on one sessions are R49 per 45 minutes — submit your problem when you book.

How do one on one sessions work?

Studio includes one free 45-minute one on one session each month. Library, Scholar, and extra Studio sessions are R49 per 45 minutes. Submit your problem when you book so the mentor can prepare.

How does the Studio showcase work?

Studio only. Up to two projects a month are featured on the website and can be shared on LinkedIn and Facebook. Companies enquire from the page. If they buy, the builder keeps 90% and KaiSync Institution keeps 10%. Under-18 students need a guardian for any sale.

What payment methods are accepted?

Card payments through PayFast, in South African Rand. Enrolment is month-to-month.

Can I cancel or change plan?

Yes. All plans are billed monthly with no lock-in. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your dashboard.

Can a company train a team?

Yes. Team seats start at R199 per person a month (minimum 5), and workshops start at R3,500. See For Business or WhatsApp +27 75 872 7511.
