About KaiSync Institution

An AI academy. Recordings first. You choose the course.

KaiSync Institution teaches practical AI — websites, automation, marketing, and applications — as an open catalog, not a lock-step term.

Our mission

Closing the gap between curiosity and capability.

KaiSync Institution exists because too many people who are curious about AI never get the chance to actually build with it. We publish five recorded courses — Basic AI; Build a website, beginning to end; Automation with Zapier, n8n, or Power Automate; Marketing with AI; and Applications. One subscription opens all of them. You pick what you want. You can sit in several at once. New to AI? Start with Basic AI. You do not have to.

How the academy runs

Catalog, plans, then showcase.

Open catalog

Library R99, Scholar R149, and Studio R249 all include every recorded course. Pick any. Join more than one.

Labs, not 1:1

Scholar: 1 live lab and 1 marked assignment a month. Studio: 2 labs and 3 project clinic reports. Private 1:1 is not in R249.

Studio showcase

Up to two Studio projects a month on the website, LinkedIn, and Facebook. Companies enquire. Builder 90%, academy 10%.

Companies and pathways

Teams can train. Top students can teach.

A company can register a group for the catalog, or book an on-site workshop on how to use AI at work. Each year KaiSync Institution invites the top two students to help teach labs. Under-18 students can be showcased with a guardian — they cannot sell a project alone.
